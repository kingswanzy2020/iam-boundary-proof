Limits and tradeoffs

The policy simulator does not make a live service request, and AWS states that results can differ from the live environment.

No resource-based policy was supplied. No service control policy, cross-account trust, or separate human console session was tested.

The proof trades those wider runtime questions for a reproducible, no-side-effect decision over the policy inputs captured in this repository.
